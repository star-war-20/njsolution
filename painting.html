FAQ

Painting & Decorating Questions

Common questions from our Glasgow customers about our painting services.

How many coats of paint do you apply?
For most interiors we apply a primer or mist coat followed by two topcoats minimum. On new plaster or darker colour changes, additional coats may be needed. We never cut corners — all surfaces are properly covered for an even, lasting finish.
Do you supply the paint or do I buy it?
Both options work for us. We can supply premium trade-grade paints at competitive prices, or work with paint you've already purchased. If you'd like advice on brand, sheen level, or coverage calculations, we're happy to help.
Can you paint exterior walls and woodwork?
Yes — exterior painting including masonry, render, fascias, soffits, and window/door frames is well within our capability. We use weather-resistant products suitable for the Scottish climate to ensure a long-lasting result.
How long does decorating a room take?
An average bedroom typically takes 1–2 days including preparation, priming, and two coats. Living rooms or rooms with extensive woodwork take a little longer. We'll give you a realistic timeframe when we assess the job.
